## What changed

- **Up to 3 Claude accounts.** In the app, tap **Add another account**. On claude.com, log out or switch to the other account first, then sign in as usual. You give each account a short name, like Personal or Work.
- **One widget, every account.** The widget shows the account's name at the top and a dot for each account at the bottom. Tap the name or the dots to go to the next account. Each widget remembers its own account, so you can also put one widget per account on your home screen.
- **Small sizes too.** At 1x1 and 2x2 the widget shows the account's first letter in a corner, and at 2x1 its name under the percent. Tapping it still switches.
- **All accounts stay fresh.** Every refresh fetches all accounts at once.
- **Tabs in the app.** Each account has its own tab, numbers, history, theme and crab skin. A new account starts with the look of the one you added it from.
- **A signed-out account warns on its own page.** Its last numbers stay, greyed, with "Sign in again" in yellow, and its dot turns yellow. Tapping it opens the app on that account. The other accounts keep working.

With only one account, Pinch looks the same as before, and your current sign-in keeps working after the update.
