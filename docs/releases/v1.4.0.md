## What changed

- **Usage alerts.** Pinch can tell you when a session reaches 80% and 95%, and when it resets. Turn them on in **Settings** in the app.
- **Update check.** Once a day Pinch asks GitHub whether a newer version is out and offers it to you. Nothing about you is sent.
- **The bug hunter crab and the Terminal theme.** A new skin that jumps to catch red butterflies with its net, and a dark terminal theme with scrolling code.
- **A tidier app.** The app now has three tabs: **Usage**, **Look** and **Settings**. Themes show a small preview of their scene, skins with their own animation are grouped first, and buttons respond to your touch.
- **Easier refresh.** The refresh button on the widget is easier to hit, and the small text is a little bigger.
- **Clearer numbers.** The big percent on the widget now says "used", so it is not mistaken for what is left.
- **Refresh fixes.** The refresh button always responds, and background refreshes keep running reliably.

Your sign-in, accounts, themes and skins carry over when you update.
