## What changed

- **The widget's refresh button refreshes.** A tap near the arrow could miss it and land on the card behind it, which opens the app. The arrow now has a much bigger touch area at the top-right corner of the widget, so a tap there refreshes in place.
- **Clawd roams the whole widget.** He used to stay in the right part. Now he trots across the full width, under the big number too, and the number keeps a soft shadow so it stays readable.

Your sign-in, accounts, themes and skins carry over when you update.
